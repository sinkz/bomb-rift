## Prompt

Create a polished game character concept sheet for the indie game BOMB RIFT, featuring THREE COMPLETELY ORIGINAL intimidating but charming bosses. This is a 2D PIXEL ART art-direction proposal for an existing dark purple pixel-art roguelike with a 3D isometric combat arena. Landscape sheet approximately 3:2 ratio, three evenly spaced columns, a single full-body boss per column, all same visual weight, no cropped feet, readable at small scale. Dark near-black violet background with extremely subtle pixel checker pattern. Beautiful disciplined pixel clusters, crisp stepped outlines, deliberate limited color ramps, rich material shading with NO smooth vector shapes, NO photorealism, NO smeared brushwork. 3/4 slightly overhead game camera. Small grounding shadow under each character. Restrained spectral sparkle accents. Clear separation between bodies and limbs, structurally attached limbs, no floating hands, no floating disconnected orbs. Give each column a restrained gold pixel heading and two lines of short legible typography.

LEFT: heading 'JARDIM VORAZ', bottom title 'BRIAROK', subtitle 'O jardim faminto'. A massive carnivorous flower-root beast, low squat four-root-foot stance, thick twisting woody limbs clearly attached to a dark knotted body; heavy crown of jagged fleshy petals in raspberry and wine red around a glowing amber toothed mouth. Moss green armor leaves and thorny shoulders. Mouth is the glowing heart of the silhouette. No human face and no bell chest. A few seed pods integrated in its back. It feels ancient, botanical and hungry.

No loose floating orbs. Low wide silhouette distinct from the flower beast. Imposing precision-machine personality.

Heavy grounded posture, NOT a humanoid, NOT a floating ice queen.
